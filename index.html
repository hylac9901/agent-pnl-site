Agent P&L

Economic control for AI agents.

Agent P&L is an early-stage Economic SLO Runtime for AI-agent workflows.

Before a paid agent action executes, Agent P&L is designed to evaluate whether its expected incremental business value justifies its marginal cost and downside.

After execution, it links the decision to actual provider cost and an independently recorded business outcome so the economic policy can be evaluated and recalibrated.

Current focus:

We are seeking one metadata-only, shadow-only design partner with repeated paid AI actions, provider-recorded cost, an independent CRM/payment/booking/ERP outcome, and stable action-to-outcome lineage.

Initial shadow evaluation does not require transcripts, audio, customer PII, production credentials, or production enforcement.
